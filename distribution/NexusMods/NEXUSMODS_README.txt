[h1]Medic Button Splint Rigging[/h1]

A simple QoL mod that adds the "Splint Rigging" job to the characters job queue when shift clicking the medic button.

[b]Requirements[/b]
[list]
[*][url=https://www.nexusmods.com/kenshi/mods/847]RE_Kenshi[/url] (tested on v0.34)
[*]Kenshi v1.0.65+
[/list]

[b]Compatibility[/b]
[list]
[*]No special load order required beyond RE_Kenshi
[*]Does not alter any game files
[/list]

[b]Troubleshooting[/b]
[list]
[*]Confirm RE_Kenshi is installed and enabled
[/list]
